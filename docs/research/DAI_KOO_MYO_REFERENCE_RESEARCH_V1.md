## 2. EXTERNAL REFERENCE CONSENSUS

Across multiple Reiki reference sources, the traditional Usui Dai Ko Myo is described as the Japanese phrase:

**大光明**

commonly romanized as **Dai Kō Myō / Dai Ko Myo** and translated along the lines of “Great Shining Light” or “Great Bright Light.”

The three characters are:
- `大` — dai — great / large;
- `光` — kō — light / radiance;
- `明` — myō — bright / clear / radiance.

External sources also show that:
- multiple calligraphic renderings circulate;
- Western transmission produced graphical variation;
- the Usui-kanji form must be distinguished from the separate Tibetan/Dumo master-symbol form used by some later systems/lineages.
