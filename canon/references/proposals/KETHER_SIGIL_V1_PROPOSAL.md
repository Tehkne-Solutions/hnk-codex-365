# KETHER SIGIL V1 — PROPOSAL

**Status:** `PROPOSAL_NOT_CANON`  
**Scope:** Portal 036 — Kether → Chokmah  
**Related:** #14, #25

> This is an HNK-original product/canonical proposal. It is not presented as a historical Kabbalistic sigil, angelic seal or recovered occult glyph.

## 1. Purpose

Define one unambiguous Kether sigil that can function as:

- Portal 036 activation surface;
- 2D master;
- motion source;
- 3D/AR derivative source;
- completion/reward mark for the Kether vertical slice.

The sigil must express Kether as origin/crown/emanation while remaining consistent with the approved HNK structural numerology.

## 2. Candidate identity

Name:

**Sigilo da Coroa de Kether — HNK V1**

Asset id:

`hnk.kether.sigil.v1.master`

## 3. Geometric grammar

The geometry uses only HNK-original primitives.

### Layer A — Origin

- one central point;
- point radius is `1` base visual unit;
- represents origin/potential before form.

### Layer B — Unity

- one unbroken inner circle around the origin;
- no text or letter inside the circle;
- represents continuity/unity.

### Layer C — Triple emanation

- three concentric rings total, including the inner circle;
- radii are proportional `1 : 2 : 3`;
- the triple ring references expansion / friction / convergence as an invisible structural principle, not as visible labels.

### Layer D — Twelve gates

- twelve radial gates distributed every 30 degrees;
- North is gate `01`;
- order proceeds clockwise through gate `12`;
- the gates correspond structurally to the approved 12 experiential movements, but the user-facing sigil does not print their names.

### Layer E — Thirty-six marks

- outermost circumference carries 36 micro-marks;
- one mark for each Day of Kether;
- every third mark aligns with one of the twelve gates;
- the 36 marks may illuminate progressively during the Kether journey.

### Layer F — Crown axis

- one vertical axis extends from the origin through North;
- at the outer boundary it terminates in a simple three-point crown form;
- the crown form is geometric, not heraldic: center apex plus two lower lateral points.

The crown axis establishes a stable top/orientation and prevents arbitrary rotation.

## 4. Orientation

The master has exactly one canonical orientation:

- crown apex at North / top;
- vertical axis at `0°`;
- no mirrored variant is canonical;
- 3D derivatives preserve front/top orientation.

## 5. Construction grid

Recommended vector master:

- square `1200 × 1200` viewBox;
- center `(600,600)`;
- base radius unit `R = 144`;
- rings at `R`, `2R`, `3R`;
- twelve gate axes at `-90° + n×30°`;
- thirty-six marks at `-90° + n×10°`.

Exact stroke widths remain design-system variables until visual-master approval.

## 6. Meaning boundary

The product may describe the geometry as:

- HNK sigil of Kether;
- completion/activation operator in the HNK model;
- visual key for Portal 036.

The product must not describe it as:

- an ancient recovered Kabbalistic seal;
- an objective detector/transmitter of supernatural forces;
- a traditional angelic sigil unless a separate historical source explicitly supports such a claim.

## 7. Portal behavior

Before Kether completion:

- outer 36 marks can reflect completed Days;
- crown axis remains dormant;
- Portal activation remains locked.

At `35/35` pre-Portal readiness:

- 35 marks are integrated;
- Portal mark `36` remains unresolved.

During Day 036:

- origin point activates first;
- three rings emerge from inside outward;
- twelve gates become perceptible;
- final 36th mark lights only after valid Portal completion;
- crown axis resolves last.

No confetti.

## 8. Reduced motion

Reduced-motion rendering uses static state changes:

`origin → rings → gates → 36th mark → crown`.

No rotation is required for comprehension.

## 9. 3D / AR derivative

Allowed:

- shallow relief;
- engraved stone/metal;
- emissive line version;
- transparent layered rings;
- controlled depth separation.

Forbidden:

- changing gate count;
- changing mark count;
- mirroring;
- rotating canonical North;
- adding unrelated occult glyphs into the master.

## 10. Visual language

Kether-specific material direction:

- black void;
- intense white light;
- aged/emissive gold as integration material;
- extreme visual restraint;
- no tarot-card framing;
- no generic magic-circle clutter.
