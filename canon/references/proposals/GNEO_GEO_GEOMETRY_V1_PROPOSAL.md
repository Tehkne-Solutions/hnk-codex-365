> This proposal creates a new HNK-original geometry from the source-backed functional ontology. It is not presented as a recovered historical Bluefluke/Gneo Geo drawing.

## 1. Purpose

Create one stable, repeatable, mobile-legible topology for the Gneo Geo Astral so Day 028 can be implemented without inventing geometry at runtime.

The geometry must preserve the source-backed invariants:

- one central command position;
- one Double Goetic Star / `Estrela Goética Dupla` operator family;
- eight Consciousness Circuits;
- intentional navigation among the eight circuits;
- a distinguishable active/control implementation;
- no claim that the diagram proves an external quantum mechanism.

## 3. Topology

### Center

One fixed center point `O` represents the **Cockpit / Command Position**.

The center is not a ninth circuit.

### Inner structure

An eight-point star is built from two interlocked squares:

- Square A oriented to North / East / South / West;
- Square B rotated exactly 45 degrees relative to Square A.

This creates an octagram with eight primary directional points.

### Outer structure

A second congruent octagram surrounds the inner octagram and is rotated exactly **22.5 degrees** relative to it.

The pair forms the HNK-original interpretation of the source term **Estrela Goética Dupla**.

No historical claim is made for this construction.

### Circuit ring

Eight circuit nodes sit on one circular ring between the inner and outer octagrams.

Each circuit node has one radial connection to the center.

The radial lines are functional navigation paths, not decorative rays.

## 4. Orientation

Orientation is fixed.

- top = North / `0°`;
- right = East / `90°`;
- bottom = South / `180°`;
- left = West / `270°`.

The diagram must never auto-rotate with device orientation.

North remains visually identifiable even when labels are hidden.

The return to `O` represents integration/command, not a ninth activation.

## 6. Interaction states

Each node supports:

- `DORMANT` — visible but inactive;
- `PERCEIVED` — user focused/hovered;
- `ACTIVE` — current circuit;
- `EXPLORED` — session evidence exists;
- `INTEGRATED` — all required circuit observations for the current Day/session are complete.

The center supports:

- `AVAILABLE`;
- `COMMAND_ACTIVE`;
- `INTEGRATION_READY`;
- `INTEGRATED`.

## 7. Day 028 active condition

The active HNK condition uses:

- center `O`;
- both octagrams;
- eight named circuit nodes;
- radial center connections;
- fixed North orientation;
- clockwise default traversal;
- return to center.

## 8. Day 028 control condition

The control condition must preserve:

- same canvas size;
- same eight node coordinates;
- same center coordinate;
- same traversal timing.

It removes:

- both octagrams;
- sacred/operator styling;
- circuit names during the active phase.

Control displays only:

- one simple circle;
- center point;
- eight numbered points `1–8`.

This maintains structural comparability while separating the HNK geometry/operator family.

## 9. Visual language

Until final asset production, this proposal freezes only topology, not final material/color.

When rendered inside Kether:

- background: Kether void;
- lines: restrained luminous/metallic material;
- center: strongest authority point;
- node activation communicates state through light/material, not color alone;
- no horror/demonic ornamentation;
- the historical label `Goética` must not drive demonic iconography.

## 10. Mobile rules

At 390 px width:

- all eight nodes remain tappable;
- labels may move to a contextual drawer instead of crowding the ring;
- the topology itself must remain unchanged;
- center and North marker remain visible;
- minimum touch target follows platform accessibility rules.

## 11. Motion

Allowed:

- line tracing from center toward current circuit;
- slow activation pulse;
- completed circuit residue;
- return-to-center convergence.

Forbidden:

- auto-spinning star;
- random rotation;
- particle storms that obscure topology;
- motion implying external entity detection.

Reduced motion:

- replace tracing with state fades;
- preserve traversal order and legibility.

## 12. 3D derivative rule

A future 3D version must preserve exactly:

- center;
- eight circuit positions;
- clockwise numbering;
- inner octagram;
- outer octagram rotation relation;
- North orientation.

Depth may be introduced only on Z-axis. Topology may not be rearranged.
