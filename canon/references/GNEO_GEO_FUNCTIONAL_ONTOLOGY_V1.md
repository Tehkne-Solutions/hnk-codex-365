Those details remain `GEOMETRY_PENDING`.

## 6. Requirements for HNK-original Gneo Geo V1 geometry

A future approval may create a new HNK-original geometry, but the proposal must:

1. visibly support all eight circuits;
2. provide one unambiguous center of command;
3. define one stable orientation;
4. define one repeatable traversal order;
5. remain legible on mobile;
6. support 2D, motion and 3D derivatives without changing topology;
7. distinguish active, inactive, explored and integrated states;
8. record provenance as **HNK-original design derived from the functional ontology**, not as recovered historical Bluefluke artwork;
9. receive version, approver and checksum before use as canonical Day 028 asset.
